==============================================================
 KEY RULES FOR TESTERS
==============================================================

1. Society OWNER (isSocietyAdmin = true):
   - Does NOT appear in the "Staff" tab inside the dashboard.
   - Can allocate/change module permissions for all staff.
   - Only the Platform SuperAdmin can manage the owner.

2. Staff (isSocietyAdmin = false):
   - Appears in the "Staff" section.
   - CANNOT change their own or others' permissions.
   - Can be deactivated/removed by the society owner.

3. Banking Heads are pre-seeded (SAV, CUR, FDR, REC, LON, PGM).
   This fixes the error: "No banking heads are configured yet".

4. Deposit Plans (in the Plan Catalogue tab):
   - Fixed Deposit (FD) and Recurring Deposit (RD) are supported.
   - Account Registry now shows ALL account types:
     Savings, Current, Fixed Deposit, RD, Pigmy, General.
